पता PATA

In Hindi, पता means both “to know” and “an address” — knowing where a child actually is.

A teacher of forty children cannot tell who understood today's lesson. PATA tells her in twenty seconds — and tells her what they got wrong, not just how many.
1

The problem

40

children in one government school classroom

7

grade levels sitting in that same room

0

minutes in her day to find out who is lost

She is not failing them — there is simply no moment in the day where finding out is possible. And a test result a week later tells her a number, never the mistake behind it.

2

The solution — report the mistake, not the mark

What every app gives her
3 / 5

True, and useless. She cannot teach a number tomorrow morning.

And it usually reaches her a week later, when the lesson has moved on.

→
What PATA gives her
“Eight children are still taking the smaller digit from the bigger one, whichever is on top.”

Every wrong option in the app is tied to one named misconception — so the result is a sentence she can put on the board.

3

How it works

1
Teacher

Ask

She reads one question aloud. The children answer A, B, C or D on their fingers — no devices in class.

2
Teacher

Tap · 20 sec

She taps whoever got it wrong on a picture of her own seating plan. One extra tap names the common wrong answer.

3
Teacher

Send home

Per-child, never an average. One tap sends exactly those children practice for the evening.

4
Child

Understand

At home they open their own app, in their own language, and the idea is explained — read aloud if they cannot read it.

5
Child

Practise

Five questions that get harder if they are getting it and easier if they are not. Answers always shown.

↺ By morning she sees, for each child by name, the mistake they are still making — and the loop starts again.
